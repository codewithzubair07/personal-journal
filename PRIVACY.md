# Privacy Policy — Personal Gemini Journal

Last updated: September 2026

Personal Gemini Journal ("the app") is a demo application built for the
Gen AI Exchange Cohort 3 Ideathon Challenge.

## What we collect
- Your Google account email and basic profile info (name, avatar), provided
  by Google Sign-In, used solely to identify your account.
- Journal entries you write and save, including AI-generated summaries,
  mood tags, and themes derived from your conversations.

## How we use it
- Your data is used only to provide the journaling and "Ask Your Journal"
  features within the app.
- Your journal entries are never shared with other users. Each user's data
  is stored in an isolated section of our database, accessible only to
  that user.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for
  advertising or any other purpose.

## Third-party services
- Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database)
- Google Gemini API (for AI conversation, summarization, and search)
